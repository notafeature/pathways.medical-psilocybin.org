What the draft asks of a healing center

Identity and structure. Legal name, address, telephone, and e-mail; primary contacts and affiliated persons; an organizational chart of governance and operations; a list of all owners or board members with contact information; a list of all other employees by legal name; primary program contact with a physical address. 7.35.3.11 (A)(1) to (3), p. 5; 7.35.3.11 (A)(10), (11), (22), p. 5

Registrations and the building. Proof of registration with the Secretary of State and with Taxation and Revenue; copies of any required city or county business licenses; a certificate of occupancy for each New Mexico location; proof of ownership of property where administration sessions will occur, or the owner's acknowledgment; proof of compliance with accessibility laws. These are the agencies and papers the producer pathway walks: the business license and the facility. 7.35.3.11 (A)(4) to (9), p. 5

Plans. Record retention; patient confidentiality, both generally and within the center; safety and emergency response for the location's settings; complaint and grievance procedures available to patients; secured storage for medical psilocybin; wastage of unused psilocybin; transparency and disclosure of fees; proof of a functioning communication device that reliably reaches emergency services. 7.35.3.11 (A)(12) to (20), p. 5

Outdoor or natural settings. A detailed description of the outdoor administration area, an emergency safety and response plan, and proof that emergency medical services can reliably be contacted from the site. 7.35.3.11 (A)(21), p. 6

Affirmations. Consent to publication of contact information, an affirmation of accuracy, and an attestation that no person associated with the applicant is a registered sex offender in any jurisdiction. 7.35.3.11 (A)(23), p. 6

Once certified. The requirements for healing centers and other approved locations are at 7.35.3.20, p. 14; department evaluation and assessment at 7.35.3.21, p. 16; the dual-ownership bar between a certificant and a permittee at 7.35.3.23, p. 16.
